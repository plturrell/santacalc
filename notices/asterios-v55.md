# Asterios Panoras’s Elf Calculator Programs and Experiments

The unmodified program bank and lineage files come from [Kaggle catalog version 55](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/asteriospanoras/santa-2026-elf-programs-and-experiments/versions/55) under [Apache 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The author’s release history labels the snapshot v53 and records 136,813 instructions. The program bank SHA-256 is `35226ecbbcf2a7c9adb8d788e77636987bf97f80ee7710f950c0473d25002dff`.

The local merge selected three programs from this bank. The program for `scored_0894` credits [Ertuğrul Özer’s Starter Notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/ozertuu/santa-2026-starter-notebook). The programs for `scored_0529` and `scored_0961` credit Asterios Panoras’s reruns of [Atakan Aldemir’s multiplier-frontier optimizer](https://www.kaggle.com/code/atakanaldemir/santa-2026-joint-recipes-for-large-puzzles) on the merged public program bank. Per-program credits remain in `best_programs.csv`.
